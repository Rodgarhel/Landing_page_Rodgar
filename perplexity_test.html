01

Open with your specialty.

Say exactly what you do in one line: 3D animation, stylized visual storytelling, motion design, rigging, or cinematic Blender work.

02

Make the reel easy to reach.

The reel should be visible in the first screen or one click away, with a clean thumbnail and a short duration label.

03

Show process, not just polish.

Studios and clients often want evidence of thinking: roughs, shots, iterations, nodes, and your contribution to the final result.

Style system

Color palette and fonts

This palette is warm and cinematic, with a deep teal accent for actions and a copper note for editorial highlights. The font pairing keeps the page stylish but still professional and readable.

Typography

Cabinet Grotesk + Satoshi

Cabinet Grotesk gives the hero and headings a contemporary editorial edge, while Satoshi keeps body copy, labels, and navigation clean and highly readable.

Contact block

Simple, direct closing CTA

End the page with a short invitation, email link, and one booking button. Avoid long forms on a portfolio landing page unless qualification is essential.
